# Idea Mining

Start from repeated limitations, disagreements, missing evaluations, failed assumptions, or important constructs that current systems do not operationalize. Keep the research question separate from a convenient implementation.

A gap is credible only when at least two independent papers expose it or a strong benchmark leaves a measured capability untested. Before retaining it, run `references/freshness-check.md`: search the claimed gap, synonyms, alternative terminology, nearest methods, relevant author/lab follow-ups, and the latest 3-6 months. Record counterexamples and narrow or reject the gap.

For each candidate provide the gap in plain language, importance, supporting and contradicting papers, nearest prior work, exact field-by-field difference, falsifiable hypothesis, competing explanation, minimum method, smallest experiment that could disprove it, required data, external validation, scope exclusions, reviewer-interest fit, likely objection, and confidence.

Reject candidates requiring a new benchmark, new model, new agent system, new infrastructure, and new deployment stack together. Prefer one central mechanism plus at most one supporting check. Require evaluation outside the project's own benchmark through a public dataset, held-out source, transfer task, or controlled domain shift.

Return at most five candidates. Rank by evidence strength, current novelty risk, implementation size, reviewer-interest fit, falsifiability, and external testability. Mark statements as direct evidence, synthesis, inference, or unverified.
