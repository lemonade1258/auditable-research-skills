# Scope and generalization gate

Before approving an idea, answer these questions:

1. Is there one central method mechanism rather than several independent systems?
2. Can the core experiment be completed with existing data and tools?
3. Does the method add at most one supporting verifier, repair loop, or abstention policy?
4. Is there a validation setting outside the user's own benchmark: public benchmark, held-out source, cross-venue split, transfer task, or controlled domain shift?
5. Can the main claim be tested with a small number of pre-registered metrics?

If any answer is no, reduce the idea before discussing implementation. A benchmark-only gain is not evidence of generalization. A method that needs a new benchmark to look successful should be treated as a benchmark contribution, not a general method contribution.
