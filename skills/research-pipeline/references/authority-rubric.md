# Authority rubric

Use these tiers when deciding whether a paper can support a novelty or method claim.

- **A**: manually verified peer-reviewed top conference/journal paper, canonical seminal paper, official benchmark paper, or authoritative standard. Use for core claims.
- **B**: peer-reviewed specialist venue or strong adjacent venue. Use for supporting claims and neighboring methods.
- **C**: preprint, workshop, technical report, repository, survey, or tool page. Use for discovery and emerging directions; do not use alone to establish novelty.
- **D**: blog, search snippet, unsourced list, or model memory. Never use as evidence.

Record the tier and why it was assigned. Do not assign A from DOI presence, citation count, or a venue-name heuristic. Manually verify publication status and venue for every core paper. Venue prestige alone does not prove method quality; inspect task, data, and evaluation.
